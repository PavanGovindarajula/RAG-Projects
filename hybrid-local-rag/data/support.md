## VPN error VPN-104
VPN-104 means the device certificate has expired. Connect to the office network, open Device Portal, and select Renew Certificate. Restart the VPN client afterward.

## Password reset
If you cannot remember your account password, visit the company password portal and choose Forgot Password. Verify your identity with your registered authenticator.

## Laptop battery
If a laptop loses charge unusually quickly, run the battery health check in Device Portal. Contact the help desk if health is below 70 percent.

## Wi-Fi error WIFI-220
WIFI-220 indicates an incorrect wireless profile. Forget the Company-Secure network, then reconnect using your company credentials.

## New employee access
A manager requests new employee application access through the Access Portal. Requests require the application owner's approval.
